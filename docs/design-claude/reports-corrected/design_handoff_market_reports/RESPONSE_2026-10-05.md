# Response to corrections — 2026-10-05

Read against `00-SHARED.md`, `01-PROPERTY.md`, `02-MARKET.md`, `03-SEND.md`. Every item is either applied in the design files in this folder, decided below, or handed back as a product question. Ratios quoted are WCAG values computed in the design file's own logic.

## Decisions you asked for

**Market running head: KEEP IT.** Both surfaces now carry the same 40px running head (report · area | date range) as the PDFShift header from page 1; the brand band on page 1 is body content below it; the only PDFShift footer starts at page 1. The pages-2+ brand strip from the earlier package is withdrawn — the running head is the continuation header. §3a of `PDF Reports v2.dc.html` is superseded on that point.

**new_listings vs new_listings_gallery: option 2, one kind with a density switch.** `Report Page.dc.html` → kind `listings`, prop `listingsDensity: grid | table`. Header and stats are identical; the body is the 3×2 photo grid or the table (Address · Bd/Ba · Sq ft · List price · Listed · Days). Naming: PDF title and both wizard screens say **"New Listings"** for the table and **"New Listings Gallery"** for the grid. Nothing is deleted from the product.

**`primary_ink` target: must clear 4.5 against white AND `tint`.** The design file's derivation loops against both; derive to that in `themes.py`.

**Dash: legible.** `#B9BBC1` is withdrawn. A dash is the value in a value cell, so it is `#5E636B` (6.05 on white) at weight 400, against 600 for known values — unknown is distinguished by weight, not by being faint. No auditor exclusion needed.

**COMP_SET_MAX = 15: page 4 carries all of them; the comps page pictures the nearest six.** Header: "15 sales within 0.75 miles · last 6 months · nearest 6 pictured, all 15 on page 4". "Each sale" bars compress to 14px / 5px gaps above 8 comps; the range panel and compare table tighten so 15 rows fit. Toggle `manyComps` in Tweaks. The report never claims to show what it doesn't.

**Narrative truncation: hard cap at generation on a sentence boundary; line-clamp as the visible signal.** The "In short" box is `-webkit-line-clamp: 4`, so an overrun ends in an ellipsis rather than a cut word.
