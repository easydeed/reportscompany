## Correction accepted: the display-size exception

You're right, and it was the same mistake as `#8A8E95`: stated, not computed. Measured today, white on the six brands: red 4.83 · teal 3.74 · cyan 5.36 · violet 5.70 · **amber 2.15 · lime 1.98**.

Fixed in both design files with a derived token rather than an exception:

`display_ink = contrast(white, primary) ≥ 3.0 ? white : on_primary`

- Market: big number, 26px stat values, band label (3 sites in `Report Page.dc.html`).
- Property: cover street line, city line, 30px stat values, next-steps panel name (4 sites in `Property Report.dc.html`).

On amber and lime the display text now takes `on_primary` (`#14151a`, 8.49 / 9.23). The brand fill still never moves. Both READMEs updated; add `display_ink` to `themes.derive_theme` next to `on_primary` and `accent_ink`. That should close the 42 of 45 remaining property failures; please re-measure.
