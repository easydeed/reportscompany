Turn 1 · PDF reports · Market Snapshot + New Listings

Two modern directions for the PDFs

One brand color, no gradient. The violet→coral bar and the red “PDF” badge go. Each page uses the agent’s own color.
One number leads. Median price is the headline, not one of four equal chips in a ribbon.
Cut the filler. “These metrics help you understand…” becomes a real market read, or nothing.
Rules instead of boxes. Hairlines and type do the work of the rounded cards and dashed tiles. A months-of-inventory scale gives the raw number meaning.
1a Editorial Serif numbers, lots of white, reads like a local market column.
